ABOUT THIS CONCEPT

Built around human help.

Linked explores support for young women and LGBTQI+ people on nights out in Eindhoven.

This prototype saves demo alerts only in your browser. It does not contact volunteers, an event desk, or emergency services. No partner participation is confirmed.

Use the Volunteer and Help point views to try the response workflow. Bracelet connectivity is simulated.
